Direct WhatsApp Ordering

PINACLE FASHIONS offers a transparent, zero-gateway personal boutique ordering experience via our official concierge at {{ WHATSAPP_DISPLAY_NUMBER }}.

  • Each order submitted at checkout records a permanent reference ID and reserves your selected inventory immediately.
  • Your pre-formatted order sheet opens on WhatsApp so our stylists can confirm delivery timelines, measurements, or custom blouse inquiries before payment coordination.
  • No credit card or automated payment credentials are ever collected on this website.

Shipping & Pan-India Delivery

Every garment is steam-finished, folded in acid-free tissue, and dispatched in our signature keepsake box.

  • Complimentary Shipping: Orders above ₹2,999 ship free across India (flat ₹99 nominal courier fee below ₹2,999).
  • Dispatch Window: Ready-to-ship ensembles dispatch within 24–48 business hours.
  • Estimated Transit: 2–4 days within Kerala and South India; 4–7 business days nationwide via insured BlueDart/DTDC partners.

Returns & Size Exchanges

We curate every saree and ethnic ensemble through strict multi-stage quality inspection prior to dispatch.

  • Size exchanges or defect claims are accepted within 7 days of delivery provided original tags, fall-pico condition, and packaging remain intact.
  • Please share a 360-degree continuous unboxing video without pauses on WhatsApp within 24 hours in case of transit damage or missing accents.
  • Custom-tailored or altered outfits are ineligible for return unless a craftsmanship flaw is present.

Silk Care & Privacy Commitment

Handloom weaves deserve mindful care to retain their natural zari lustre across generations.

  • Garment Care: Strictly dry clean pure Kanjeevaram, Banarasi, Chanderi, and embroidered organza silk creations. Store wrapped in unbleached muslin away from direct sunlight.
  • Data Privacy: Your delivery address and phone number are used exclusively to process your PINACLE FASHIONS order and courier updates. We never sell or share client data.
